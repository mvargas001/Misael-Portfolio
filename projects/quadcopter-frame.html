01 · Overview

Overview

Decurion is a manned-unmanned teaming system in active development. The simulation side, built in ArduPilot SITL and running through Mission Planner, is working: I can command two drones by voice, and one follows the other autonomously. Both physical drones are now assembled and in flight testing.

02 · Problem & Approach

Problem & Approach

The problem

Drone operations are bottlenecked by a 1:1 ratio — one operator, one vehicle. As defense and commercial missions move toward multiple assets, that ratio doesn't scale. The bottleneck isn't the drones; it's command and coordination.

Decurion is built around a ground station that tracks every vehicle, relays position and state between them, and lets one person direct the whole team. Voice is how commands get in; the real work is routing each command to the right vehicle and keeping followers in sync with their leader.

The approach

  1. Command spine — a single-drone toolkit (connect, arm, take off, go to, land) that every later layer builds on.
  2. Voice + routing — offline speech recognition and a two-stage parser: pick the drone, then the action.
  3. Teaming — the ground station relays the leader's position to the follower in real time.
  4. Real hardware — move each layer onto physical drones, one drone at a time.

Every layer is proven in SITL first — logic that looks clean in simulation doesn't always survive real radios, latency, and timing.

    In progress
    Debugging case study

    Runaway climb in Loiter mode

    Problem
    The drone climbed erratically and ignored throttle inputs in Loiter mode.
    What the log shows
    The flight controller's altitude input (green) overshot the commanded altitude (red) due to a massive spike in the raw barometer reading (orange). ArduPilot's EKF-fused estimate (yellow) failed to filter it, corrupting the controller's altitude perception.
    Root cause
    Turbulent prop wash disrupting the unshielded barometer.
    Fix
    Open-cell foam over the sensor — blocks direct airflow while still letting ambient pressure equalize.
